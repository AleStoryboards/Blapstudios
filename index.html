Why it sticks

Memorability isn't luck. It's built in.

Both systems are built around how people actually pay attention and remember. That's what makes the work stick instead of scroll past.

①

Motion is seen first

We notice movement before we read a single word. Animation wins the first three seconds, where attention is decided.

②

Distinctiveness is remembered

The brain encodes what stands out and discards what blends in. A distinctive animated world is what gets stored, and remembered brands are the ones that get chosen.

③

Emotion locks it in

Emotion is the brain's save button. Story and character make people feel something, and feeling is what turns a view into recall.
